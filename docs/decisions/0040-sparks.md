# 0040 — Sparks: every What-if, free, on every check, in its own tab

Recorded 2026-11-09.

- **The panel offers two or three "What if…" ideas on every check**, strong idea or weak (the
  prefill prompt; `WhatIfPolicy.shows` is always true). They come in the same call as the rest
  of the panel's take, about half a cent a check, paid from margin.
- **Free on every plan.** `PlanFeature.whatIf` is included in Free; the Brief shows all of
  them; Plus no longer lists them. `docs/PRICING.md` updated.
- **A Sparks tab** (Home · Ideas · Sparks · Journal): every live idea's latest check with its
  What-ifs, newest first, under the idea's title (which opens its Brief). Each Spark shows the
  what-if, the weakness it raises, why, the first test, and **Make this its own idea**, which
  saves an unchecked sibling idea (decision 0024); once made, **Open the idea**. Running the
  panel on that idea costs a check, like any idea.
- Cards keep the panel's best What-if (decision 0038).
- Existing checks have no Sparks until they are checked again.
